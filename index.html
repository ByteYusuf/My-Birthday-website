Surah Maryam 19:33

وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَيَّ يَوْمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوْمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوْمَ أُبْعَثُ حَيًّا

“And peace is upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am raised alive.”

A calm reminder that every stage of life is held in peace.

A letter to myself

Bismillah.

Another year, Alhamdulillah. I’m still here, and that alone is something to be grateful for.

This past year wasn’t always smooth. There were days I felt stuck, days things just didn’t click, and times I wanted to rush ahead of where I was. But I kept showing up, kept learning, and kept fixing what broke.

This year, I want to be kinder to myself when I’m slow, and more serious about my time. I want to keep building and keep learning, and keep my heart clean while I do it. I want to be good to the people around me, not just good at what I do.

Dua For Myself: Ya Allah, thank You for this life, my health, and every day You have given me. Forgive me for my mistakes and my heedlessness. Bless my time, my knowledge and my work. Make me patient with what I’m still waiting for, and grateful for what I already have. Protect me and the people I love. Guide me to what is best for me in this world and the hereafter, and let me live and die in Your pleasure. Ameen. 🤲

Happy birthday to me. 🎂

May this year bring me barakah, knowledge and peace. 🤲
